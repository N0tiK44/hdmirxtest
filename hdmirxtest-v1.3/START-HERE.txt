HDMIRXTEST V1.3
================

INSTALL ON THE WORKING ZOWIE

       bash TxTest.sh install

V1.3 automatically tests 1080p60, 720p60, 1080p50, 720p50 and the display's
preferred mode when those modes are present in the display EDID.

TEST A TV

1. Let the Zowie test finish.
2. Run: sudo poweroff
3. Leave HDMI-RX empty.
4. Connect only HDMI-TX to the TV.
5. Select that HDMI input and power on the Pi.
6. Watch for colour bars for at least three minutes.
7. Power off and reconnect the Zowie.

Every boot is permanently saved. Zowie boots cannot overwrite TV evidence.

COLLECT RESULTS ON THE ZOWIE

       bash TxTest.sh history
       bash TxTest.sh status
       bash TxTest.sh results

Result archive:

       /home/visionseek/hdmirxtest-tx-history-latest.tar.gz

Do not run setup.sh and do not connect HDMI-RX until direct HDMI-TX works on
the Zowie, LG and Samsung.
