============================

Custom EDID source
------------------
The downstream Zowie XL2546X EDID was recovered from the DRM connector data in
the supplied Orange Pi debug archive. The recovered source is exactly 256 bytes
and both 128-byte checksums are valid.

Hardware status
---------------
The custom EDID has not yet been electrically tested on the Orange Pi HDMI-RX.
V1.1.2 proves that negotiation data is internally valid and safely reversible;
the next returned prepare/probe archives must prove whether the RK3588 receiver
controller and driver lock to and sustain 1080p240.
